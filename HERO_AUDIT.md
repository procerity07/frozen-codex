# Hero audit report — 2026-11-23

Scope: all 73 heroes in the app (48 original + 25 two-to-four-star heroes added from the wiki). Facts were checked against the Puzzles & Chaos community wiki (puzzles-chaos.fandom.com), read **through search-engine extracts**, because direct page access was blocked in this environment. No in-game screenshots or official 37GAMES hero data were available. Treat "verified" below as "confirmed by the community wiki as of its last edit (~116 days before the check)", not as confirmed in-game.

**Combat role:** no hero received Damage, Support or Healer. The wiki's march "troop skill" text ("Deals damage equals X% of troop ATK") is boilerplate repeated across many hero pages, including Civil heroes, so it is not evidence of a hero's march role. Every hero shows "Not verified".

**Tier ratings** have been removed from the app entirely (data, types and screens).

**Not verified for the original 48 heroes:** title, march skill names/descriptions, combat role and most notes. These were not found in any source checked. They are kept and marked, not deleted. The 25 added heroes contain only wiki-listed facts (no titles, roles or invented skills).
